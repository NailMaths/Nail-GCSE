Common questions

Everything you need to know.

Which exam board does this cover?

AQA (8300) and Edexcel (1MA1) — together covering around 95% of GCSE maths students in England. Both Foundation and Higher tiers, all three papers. The course maps your child to the right board and tier from lesson one.

My child sits OCR or another board — is this still useful?

GCSE Maths subject content is set by the Department for Education and is almost entirely common across all boards. The topics, the maths, and the method are the same regardless of board. The only board-specific elements are the AQA and Edexcel mark scheme notation — OCR students will benefit from everything except those specific marking details.

My child has really struggled with maths. Will this work?

Yes — Module 00 starts before any GCSE content, covering the method, mindset, and how marks actually work. The effortful → automatic approach is specifically designed for students who feel behind and haven't responded to conventional revision.

Is this a replacement for school or a supplement?

A supplement — a powerful one. It works alongside school, filling the gap between what's covered in class and what gets marked in the exam. Most useful in the evenings and during revision periods.

What if it doesn't suit them?

There's a 14-day money-back guarantee. If your child starts the course and it isn't the right fit, contact us within 14 days for a full refund. No questions asked, no hoops to jump through.

How is this different from Corbettmaths or Mr Barton?

Free resources are libraries — useful if you know what to look up. This is a structured course: every topic in the right order, weighted by how often it appears across AQA and Edexcel papers. The workbook builds active practice against the real mark scheme. The AI tutor explains anything that isn't clicking. That combination doesn't exist in any free resource.
